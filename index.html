FAQ

Questions founders ask before the first call

Still unsure? Send Francisca a message on WhatsApp and get a direct answer.

Who do you typically work with?

Founders, SMEs and corporate teams who have outgrown spreadsheets or a part-time bookkeeper and need reliable numbers, timely tax filings and a finance partner for growth decisions.

How do I book a consultation?

Send Francisca a message on WhatsApp at 0806 464 7332, or email breezefinancemain@gmail.com. We will agree a time and review your books, tax position and cash flow together, with no obligation to engage us afterwards.

Can you clean up books that are months behind?

Yes. Catch-up bookkeeping is one of the most common starting points. We reconcile past periods, correct errors and bring your records up to date before moving you onto a monthly routine.

How is pricing structured?

Most clients are on a fixed monthly retainer based on transaction volume and scope. Project work such as system setup or investment readiness is quoted upfront.

Is my financial information kept confidential?

Always. Engagements are covered by a confidentiality agreement, and documents are shared through secure, access-controlled channels.
